How can I explore the budget and have a voice?

The budget is public at every stage. Interactive tools, public meetings, and open documents give residents real opportunities to explore the numbers, follow the process, and share their priorities before decisions are final.

How do I get involved?

Select a question to see how residents can explore, follow, and participate.

Where can I see the numbers myself?

Browse departments, funds, and projects directly.

The full budget book breaks down every department, fund, capital project, and financial schedule so residents can look beyond the summary and into the detail.

How do I find upcoming meetings?

Check the budget calendar.

The budget calendar lists workshops, public hearings, and key dates in the annual process, from the first budget instructions to final adoption.

Can I speak at a budget meeting?

Yes. Public comment is part of every hearing.

Workshops and public hearings include time for residents to ask questions and share priorities before the Board of County Commissioners makes final decisions.

Where do I find definitions of budget terms?

The glossary explains the terminology.

A glossary of budget terms and frequently asked questions helps translate financial language like millage, surtax, and fund balance into plain terms.

How do I compare this year to past years?

Financial schedules and forecasts show the trend.

Consolidated financial schedules and the multi-year financial forecast let residents see how revenue, spending, and reserves have changed over time.

Who do I contact with questions or feedback?

The Board and County staff are reachable year-round.

Residents can contact the Board of County Commissioners or the Office of Management and Budget with questions at any point in the budget cycle, not only during hearings.
