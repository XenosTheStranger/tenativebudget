What the public receives from the tentative budget

Each program combines the tentative budgets of the offices principally responsible for that work. Open a program to see the departments or agencies included, the revenue assigned to them, and the FY 2027 performance targets currently available.

How to read the costs

Amounts are department-budget context, not an audited cost allocation. Internal transfers are excluded where practicable, and a department or agency is counted in only one program to prevent duplication.
