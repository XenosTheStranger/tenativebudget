What does the County do for me?

It touches nearly every part of daily life. Walton County plans, funds, and delivers the services that keep the community safe, connected, and running — from emergency response and roads to parks, permits, and public records. Much of that work happens quietly in the background until you need it.

How does the County keep me safe?

Emergency response and public safety, every day.

Roads, bridges, and drainage connect the county.

Roads, bridges, drainage, sidewalks, and traffic improvements connect homes, schools, businesses, and neighborhoods across Walton County.

Where do residents gather and connect?

Libraries, parks, and public spaces.

Libraries, parks, recreation spaces, beaches, and public buildings give residents places to learn, gather, and enjoy time together.

Who helps with everyday needs like records or housing?

Veteran services, housing help, and civic services.

Veteran services, housing assistance, public records, elections, and community partnerships help residents navigate important moments.

Who reviews new development?

Planning, permits, and inspections.

Planning, permits, inspections, and community standards help new development happen safely and responsibly as the county grows.

Who cares for the environment we share?

Waste services and environmental stewardship.

Waste services, mosquito control, beach care, and environmental work protect the natural surroundings that everyone shares.

How does a service reach you?

  1. A need arisesA resident calls 911, drives a road, visits a park, or applies for a permit.
  2. A department respondsThe relevant County department is staffed and equipped to provide that service.
  3. The budget funds the workThe adopted budget pays for the people, equipment, and materials involved.
  4. The service is deliveredCrews respond, roads are maintained, applications are reviewed.
  5. The community benefitsDaily life stays safe, connected, and dependable.

None of this happens automatically: these services rely on a plan that is renewed every year — the County budget.
