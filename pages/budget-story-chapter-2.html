Why does the County need a budget?

Because the County cannot spend public money without a plan. Each year, Walton County must decide how much money may come in, which services need funding, and what can be completed with the money available. The budget puts those decisions in one public plan before the money is spent.

Start with the big question

What does the budget actually do?

Select a question to see how the budget affects County services and residents.

1Can the County spend whatever it wants?

No. The budget sets the limit.

The County Commission approves how much may be spent and what it may be used for. Departments must work within that approved plan unless a change is considered through the required public process.

2How does the County keep services running?

It plans for the full year.

Deputies, ambulances, roads, parks, libraries, inspections, and other services need people, fuel, equipment, buildings, and contracts. The budget makes sure those costs are considered before the year begins.

3What happens when there is not enough money for everything?

Leaders must make choices.

Needs and requests can be larger than the money available. The budget process compares them, sets priorities, and shows which work will be funded now, delayed, changed, or left out.

4How can residents see what was decided?

The plan is made public.

The budget shows the services, projects, staffing, and spending approved for the year. Public workshops and hearings give residents a chance to follow the decisions before the final budget is adopted.

5Why plan beyond this year?

Some decisions last for years.

A road, building, vehicle, or major technology project may take several years to complete and maintain. Planning ahead helps the County understand both today’s price and the costs that follow.

6What about storms and unexpected costs?

The County must be ready.

Not every need can be predicted. The budget can set aside money and protect financial flexibility so essential services can continue when emergencies or unexpected costs occur.

One year at a glance

How does a need become part of the budget?

  1. Understand the needDepartments review services, community needs, commitments, and expected costs.
  2. Estimate the moneyThe County estimates taxes, fees, grants, and other funding expected during the year.
  3. Make choicesRequests are compared with available money and County priorities.
  4. Adopt the planThe County Commission considers the budget in public and approves the final spending plan.
  5. Track the resultsSpending and collections are monitored throughout the year.

The budget answers two questions together: what services will the County provide, and where will the money come from to pay for them?
