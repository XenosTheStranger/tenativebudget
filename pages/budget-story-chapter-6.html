Why does the budget change?

Population growth, inflation, development, tourism, legislation, emergencies, grants, and changing community priorities all shape the budget. Budgets evolve because communities evolve.

GrowthMore residents and visitors can increase both revenue and demand for services.
CostsLabor, fuel, materials, insurance, and construction prices change over time.
PrioritiesNew risks and opportunities shift what the community needs most.
