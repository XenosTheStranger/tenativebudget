Statement of Function

Plans, prepares, monitors, and explains Walton County’s budget.

The Office of Management and Budget (OMB) plays a pivotal role in Walton County by providing comprehensive financial and administrative support to the Board of County Commissioners (BCC). The department is responsible for overseeing all funds authorized to ensure their proper utilization, and offers advice and guidance to department and division heads. Within the department, key functions include preparing and monitoring the annual operating and capital budget, overseeing grant budgets and reporting, and maintaining the inventory of capital assets. By diligently executing these responsibilities, the OMB contributes significantly to the County’s fiscal responsibility and overall operational efficiency, in alignment with BCC policies and regulatory standards.

Collaboration: OMB works across the County organization on budgeting, purchasing, personnel, grants, capital projects, and financial reporting.

Challenge: OMB must coordinate a growing and increasingly complex county budget while revenue conditions, operating costs, capital needs, and department priorities continue to change. The department must also maintain accurate forecasts, meet legal and GFOA reporting expectations, modernize financial tools, and explain complex decisions clearly with a limited team and fixed budget calendar.
