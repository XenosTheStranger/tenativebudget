Debt and Financial Forecast

Financial Forecast

The financial forecast presents a planning view of major fund revenues, expenditures, and fund balance from FY 2027 through FY 2031. Forecast years apply documented growth assumptions to the FY 2027 baseline and are intended for long-range planning rather than appropriation authority.

Long-Term Outlook

How current decisions affect future budgets

This is a baseline planning forecast, not a promise of future appropriations. It shows the direction of major funds if the FY 2027 tentative budget and documented assumptions continue.

Structural balance

Annual revenues and expenditures should be read together. A fund that repeatedly spends more than its recurring revenue will draw down fund balance even when its total ending balance remains positive.

Fund balance is not all available

Projected ending fund balance may include restricted, committed, assigned, or otherwise limited resources. The Tourist Development forecast separately presents the Board-committed Beach Renourishment balance and the amount available for other eligible Tourist Development uses.

Continuing obligations

Personnel, contracts, operating costs, and the General Fund support required by the Sheriff continue beyond FY 2027. The Sheriff transfer is forecast at the amount needed after applying projected Sheriff Fund revenue.

Capital and deferred costs

Capital project spending follows the five-year Capital Improvement Plan. Future operating or maintenance costs are included only when they are already part of a department’s forecast baseline; the absence of a separate amount does not mean a project has no future operating impact.

Forecast uncertainty

The forecast presents one planning baseline. Economic conditions, taxable values, tourism, state-shared revenue, grants, emergencies, inflation, project schedules, and Board policy decisions may produce different results.

How the outlook is monitored

Actual revenue, expenditures, transfers, staffing decisions, capital schedules, and fund balances should be compared with this forecast during each annual budget cycle. Assumptions are updated when newer information becomes available.

Important: This forecast does not itself determine compliance with the County’s fund-balance policy and does not replace the legally adopted annual budget. See Financial Policies, Fund Financial Schedules, and the Capital Improvement Plan.
