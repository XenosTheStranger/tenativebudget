How are budget decisions made?

The budget comes from a structured public process: department requests, staff recommendations, public workshops, and final decisions by the Board of County Commissioners.

RequestDepartments identify service needs, costs, commitments, and priorities.
ReviewStaff tests assumptions and balances requests against available resources.
AdoptThe Board holds public hearings before approving the final budget.
